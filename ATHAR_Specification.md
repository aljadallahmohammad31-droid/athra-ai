# ATHAR Platform - Technical Specifications & Pitch Guide

## Overview
ATHAR is an AI-powered customs intelligence and requirement impact governance platform designed for government sectors and enterprise business analysts.

## Key Features
1. **Cinematic Authentication & Security:** Secure SSO and encrypted login gateway.
2. **Interactive Value Proposition Hub:** Dynamic particle background introducing the platform's value for Business Analysts and government digital transformation.
3. **Core Impact Analysis Workspace:** Fully interactive strategy dependency graph, overall risk scoring (78/100), effort & SLA estimator, what-if scope simulator, departmental sign-off tracking, and automated UAT test generation.
